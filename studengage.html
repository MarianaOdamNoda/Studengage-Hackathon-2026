    01/The problem

    Students can't find what their own university already publishes.

    Specific customer

    A second-year undergraduate

    Enrolled at a Nova Scotia university, living off campus, working part-time around a fixed class timetable.

    • Not an applicant — this is student life, not recruitment
    • The buyer is the university's student-services office

    Recurring difficulty

    The same hunt, every cycle

    It restarts from nothing each time, across eight disconnected channels.

    • Events — every week
    • Bursary deadlines — every term
    • Co-op postings — every hiring round

    Why it matters

    Opportunity quietly expires

    Funding unclaimed. Placements missed. Professional networks left thin.

    • The university's outreach lands in the wrong inboxes
    • Dissatisfaction is the symptom — the cost is what nobody saw in time

    02/The solution/clear workflow

    Six steps — and the university keeps publishing exactly as it does today.

    1. 01

      University publishes

      Staff post once, by form or CSV upload: the same text they already write. Nothing is scraped.

    2. 02

      Student declares a profile

      Program, year, campus, hours free, interests. Five fields.

    3. 03

      Filter, then rank

      Not eligible or clashes with class? Out. The rest is scored, and every card shows why.

    4. 04

      Student gets a short feed

      Only what applies, each with its deadline, and at most five alerts a week.

    5. 05

      Student acts

      Applies, attends or claims — on the university's own page.

    6. 06

      The feed sharpens

      Only from what the student chooses to tell us: Interested or Not interested. No silent tracking.

    02/The solution/AI & value

    The engine ranks and matches. That is the entire job.

    What we built · role for AI

    • Built: a ranking engine you can read. Interest 35 · schedule 20 · deadline 15 · distance 10 · preferred 10 · peers 10
    • Distinctive: it knows the timetable. It hides what clashes with class, allows travel time between campuses, and says "3 of 4 sessions fit"
    • Where AI fits next: reading free-text postings into tags and criteria, so staff don't fill in fields. The prototype runs no AI model yet, by design: every rank can be explained
    • It never writes content, judges eligibility, or acts for the student

    Convincing value

    • Student: five minutes of setup replaces a weekly hunt — the bursary deadline arrives before it closes
    • University: announcements it already pays to publish finally land. No new channels, no new staff, no new content
    • Nothing depends on new behaviour. The change sits in the middle — cheapest to add, easiest to remove

    03/Privacy, outcome & next

    Private by not collecting. Measured from day one.

    • No health information. Nothing we hold falls within PHIA's scope, because we never ask for it.

    • Student-declared fields only — visible to the student, editable any time.

    • Only the university's own postings. No scraped data, no purchased data.

    • Equity self-ID is opt-in and used only to check eligibility, never to rank. No brokers, no resale.

    • Reversible consent. One stated purpose, plus export and delete on request.
